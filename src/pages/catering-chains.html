The challenge

The Zipli way

Every site feeds one portfolio-level dashboard: an audit-ready trail you can hand to leadership, or to the client whose contract you are trying to keep.

In the kitchen

Report in under 20 seconds

Reporting that is a by-product, not a project

Logged data becomes an audit-ready export for CSRD and ESRS E5. Nobody assembles it at the end of the year, because it was assembled as the shifts happened.

Serving waste across every location in one view, so sites can be compared rather than reported on one at a time.

Real-time volumes, costs and causes
