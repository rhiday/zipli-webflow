For the ESG or CR lead

The challenge

CSRD ESRS E5 makes food waste reporting mandatory for large operators. Right now that number is mostly an estimate, and an estimate does not survive an audit.

The Zipli way

Every site feeds one portfolio-level dashboard: an audit-ready trail you can hand to leadership, or to the client whose contract you are trying to keep.

In the kitchen

What the app takes off the shift

Everything a kitchen has to do around surplus lives in one app, so nothing depends on remembering to call the terminal.

Report in under 20 seconds

Take a photo of the dish and the app recognises the name, category, weight and serving date.

“Nothing to donate today” in one tap

No calls or messages to the terminal. Reporting a quiet day is a single button press.

Container requests in the app

Ask for more GN containers directly, and the terminal brings replacements on the next run.

In the ESG report

Reporting that is a by-product, not a project

Logged data becomes an audit-ready export for CSRD and ESRS E5. Nobody assembles it at the end of the year, because it was assembled as the shifts happened.

Chain-level visibility

Serving waste across every location in one view, so sites can be compared rather than reported on one at a time.

Real-time volumes, costs and causes

Not a monthly summary. The numbers move as the kitchens log, which is what makes them usable for steering.

Tools for steering operations

Order-quantity optimisation and site comparison, so the data turns into a decision rather than a chart.

An audit trail behind every figure

Trace a reported total back to the individual entry that produced it, without rebuilding anything in a spreadsheet.
