Getting set up and using it daily

The high-level version is above. Here is the step-by-step version, the kind a new kitchen or terminal actually needs on the first day.

Up and running in minutes

  1. Download the Zipli app from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS).
  2. Open it and tap “Create new account”.
  3. Enter your organisation’s details and select “I have a partnership with an existing waste terminal”, then choose your terminal from the list.
  4. Add the contact person’s details and choose a password.
  5. Read the privacy notice and accept the privacy policy.
  6. Tap “Done”. The account is ready to use immediately.

Logging a donation

  1. Tap “Log food”.
  2. Place the GN container on the scale and take a photo, or enter the details by hand.
  3. The app reads the scale, recognises the dish and fills in the name, category, weight and serving date. Check it, add any allergens, and tap “Save”.
  4. Choose why the waste happened. This stays with your own kitchen’s records, not the terminal, and tap “Save” again.

Once it’s saved

  1. The donation is sent to the terminal automatically and appears on the app’s home screen, where it stays for 48 hours.
  2. Nothing to donate today? “Nothing to donate today” on the home screen tells the terminal straight away, no call needed.
  3. Need more containers? “Need more containers?” on the home screen asks the terminal to bring more.

What can be donated, and how pickup works

General guidelines. Your terminal partner may set its own specifics.

Hot food

Held at a minimum of +60 °C throughout service, then chilled to +6 °C within 4 hours the same day.

Cold food

Salads (excluding green lettuce salad) and grated vegetables, held at +6 °C throughout service.

Also suitable

Breads, filled rolls and baguettes, pastries and desserts, milk and juices, cold cuts, canned goods, cold sauces and other dairy products.

Pickup

Food goes into the terminal’s plastic GN container, which is placed on the scale and logged in the app. The terminal collects it the next day in cold-chain transport boxes and leaves a new box and containers in exchange.
