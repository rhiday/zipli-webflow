How Turku, Helsinki, and the EU directive are reshaping food redistribution

Two Finnish cities are already building what the 2030 targets will require of everyone else.

Ninja Fedy

19 August 2026 · 4 min read

Finnish municipalities are navigating a significant shift in how food waste is governed, measured, and reported.

The September 2025 amendment to the EU Waste Framework Directive established binding food waste reduction targets for all EU Member States: a 30% per capita reduction at distribution and consumption stages by 2030. Finland designated its competent national authority by the January 2026 deadline. The regulatory framework is now in place.

What Helsinki did

Helsinki’s Testbed innovation programme funded a 21-day pilot with Stadin Safka to test whether digital coordination tools could improve the efficiency and data quality of hub operations. Results from the pilot: coordinators estimated saving around 34 hours a month, real-time donor visibility, and a documented data record of every donation received, tracked, and redistributed.

The pilot produced something more valuable than operational efficiency: verifiable impact data. The kind of data that a city sustainability office can use in its reporting to national authorities and to the EU.

What Turku did

Turku’s Bloomberg Mayors Challenge win was specifically for a project to develop digital coordination infrastructure for food redistribution. The subsequent market consultation (tietopyyntö) described the operational requirements with precision that matches Zipli’s current product exactly. The city does not need to commission a new build. The infrastructure exists.

What this means for other Finnish cities

The pattern being established in Helsinki and Turku is replicable. Cities that invest in digital redistribution coordination now are building infrastructure that serves three purposes simultaneously: operational efficiency for hub operators, a growing donor network for catering surplus, and a verified data foundation for EU food waste compliance reporting.
