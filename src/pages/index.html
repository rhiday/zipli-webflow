There’s a mismatch in food systems

By 2030, the EU requires food businesses to cut food waste by 30%, but few have a clear way to get there. Restaurants and catering kitchens have no easy way to capture surplus data in real time, understand why it happens, or donate what’s left over. Redistribution hubs are struggling with scaling their operations to reach the growing number of people who need food aid.

How can Zipli help?

Restaurants & Catering

Know your waste. Reduce it. Donate the rest.

Zipli isn’t just another way to track waste. It’s a way to act on it. Replace guesswork with a real-time, portfolio-wide view of what’s wasted and why, plus a direct route to donate what’s unavoidable, automatically counted toward the EU’s 2030 target. The result: real numbers, and real stories, to show your social and environmental impact.

Redistribution Hubs

Match more. Increase reach. Reduce admin.

Zipli helps to coordinate donations at scale. Replace phone calls and spreadsheets with a real-time view of who has surplus and who needs it, matched automatically, plus the reporting your stakeholders expect, without extra admin work. Cut time spent on core coordination tasks by ~70%, so you can focus efforts on evaluating where the need is highest, and help more people.
