The Zipli way

For the municipality or parish

The challenge

Funding a terminal usually means trusting reports built by hand, where they exist at all. Hard to show impact, and harder to justify next year’s budget on it.

The Zipli way

A live, exportable record: kilos saved, active donors, redistribution reach. No report assembled by hand, and no waiting on a coordinator’s spare time.

Three things that stop living in a spreadsheet

Incoming batches in real time

Batch details, messages and container requests appear the moment a kitchen logs them.

Structured recipient profiles

One maintained recipient registry in place of scattered contact lists.

Outcome tracking

Know how much food reaches people in need, and report it reliably.

Your side of the same three steps

1

It arrives

A kitchen logs surplus and the batch shows up in your view straight away, with the messages and container requests that came with it.

2

It gets matched

Suitable recipients are suggested from the registry you maintain, so the match is a choice between structured profiles rather than a round of phone calls.

3

It is accounted for

The pickup is recorded against the batch, so how much food reached people in need is documented rather than remembered.
