Reporting and funding

Funding a terminal usually means trusting reports built by hand, where they exist at all. Impact is hard to show, and next year’s budget is harder to justify on it.

Every donation is recorded as it happens, so kilos saved, active donors and redistribution reach are current and exportable at any moment. No report assembled by hand, and no waiting on a coordinator’s spare time.

Your side of the same three steps

1

It arrives

A kitchen logs surplus and the batch shows up in your view straight away, with the messages and container requests that came with it.

2

It gets matched

Suitable recipients are suggested from the registry you maintain, so the match is a choice between structured profiles rather than a round of phone calls.

3

It is accounted for

The pickup is recorded against the batch, so how much food reached people in need is documented rather than remembered.
