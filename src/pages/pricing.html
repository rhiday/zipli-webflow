Questions

What people ask about pricing

Is the app really free?

Yes. Logging and donating is free of charge for donor restaurants. What is priced is the multi-site data platform on top of it.

What if we run more than one site?

The app stays free for every kitchen. Comparing sites and steering the whole chain from one place is the data layer, which is priced per site.

Why is there no terminal price?

Because terminals vary enormously in intake volume and in how much of the work is volunteer time. We scope it against your own numbers in the demo rather than publishing a figure that would not fit anyone.
